# Salon AI Prompt Templates

Use these prompts with Copilot or an AI agent to generate salon-specific recommendations and messaging.

## 1) Hair service recommendation

Prompt:

"Act as a premium hair salon AI stylist. Recommend services from the salon catalog based on the following customer profile: Name: Lan, hair condition: dry and damaged, goal: soft brown balayage, budget: under 2,500,000 VND. Return valid JSON only with fields: styling_advice, recommended_services, home_care_tips. Use only real services from the catalog and never invent new services."

## 2) Post-care message

Prompt:

"Write a warm, premium Zalo message for customer Minh after a haircut and color service. The tone should be elegant, friendly, and professional. Include care instructions for the next 48 hours and a gentle reminder to book a maintenance appointment. Output JSON with fields: message_type, channel, title, message_content."

## 3) Customer history summary

Prompt:

"Summarize the customer hair history for the stylist before the next appointment. Highlight preferred colors, previous treatments, and technical care notes. Return JSON only with fields: summary, preferred_styles_or_colors, technical_notes."

## 4) AI booking assistant

Prompt:

"You are the booking assistant for THEMANH SALON. Help the receptionist suggest the best stylist and service combination for a customer who wants a haircut plus treatment. Consider hair condition, available time, and style goal. Respond in concise Vietnamese and keep the output structured."

## 5) Salon brand prompt

Prompt:

"As an expert salon marketing assistant, write a premium brand description for THEMANH SALON. Emphasize luxury, healthy hair, professional stylists, and personalized consultation. Keep under 120 words."
