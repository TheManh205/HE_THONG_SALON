# Banner Design Prompt Templates

Use these prompts with your Copilot/agent to generate banner concepts, HTML+Tailwind snippets, and image specs.

## Simple Social Banner

Prompt:

"Design a 1200x628 Facebook cover for a modern salon named 'THEMANH SALON'. Use a warm gradient (soft peach to blush), large headline 'Grand Opening', subhead 'Special offers this weekend', and a prominent CTA 'Book Now'. Output:
- A short creative brief (2-3 sentences)
- A Tailwind-compatible HTML snippet for the banner (responsive)
- PNG export spec (filename and dimensions)
" 

## Marketing Hero (Website)

Prompt:

"Create a website hero for THEMANH SALON with dark background, soft glowing accents, and headline 'Xin chào, {name}!' (replace {name} with a dynamic prop). Provide:
- Tailwind `class` based HTML/JSX component
- Suggested color variables to add to `tailwind.config.js`
- Two CTA button variants (primary and ghost)
"

## Variants Request

Prompt:

"Generate three visual variants for the hero above: (1) Minimal monochrome, (2) Photo-based with overlay, (3) Illustrated/abstract. For each variant provide a short rationale and the JSX snippet.
"
